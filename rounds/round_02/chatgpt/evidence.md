**Verification discipline.** `VERIFIED (primary abstract)` means I opened the original article's PubMed abstract in Round 02; `VERIFIED (primary full text)` means I opened the original publisher/full-text page in Round 02 and saw the claim there. `UNVERIFIED` means the *specific value or procedural detail* was not accessible from a primary source, even if an earlier agent, review, or search extract quoted it. A DOI/PMID verifies a publication's identity, not a model's frozen external validation. Source links are included for manual audit. No sample or cohort described here is claimed independent of the present data without a run-ID-level overlap audit.

## 2. Confounding and re-analysis

- **VERIFIED (primary abstract)** Yap et al. 2021, DOI **10.1016/j.cell.2021.10.015**, PMID **34767757** ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34767757/)): reported autism-related dietary preference as a mediator of ASD-associated microbiome variation. - **VERIFIED (primary full text)** Xu et al. 2019, DOI **10.3389/fpsyt.2019.00473**, PMID **31404299**, [Frontiers full article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2019.00473/full): small observational studies and methodological heterogeneity undermine stable genus-level directions.
- **UNVERIFIED (primary full text could not be opened this round; original-table search extract only)** Iglesias-Vázquez et al. 2020, DOI **10.3390/nu12030792**, PMID **32192218**, [original paper indexed Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7146354/): extreme I² in individual studies; see taxa below. This is a pooled relative-abundance analysis, not an adjusted independent cohort classification.
- **VERIFIED (primary abstract)** Andreo-Martínez et al. 2022 (online 2021), DOI **10.1007/s10803-021-05002-y**, PMID **33948825**, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33948825/): a subsequent meta-analysis. - **VERIFIED (primary abstract)** Bašić-Čičak 2024 (DOI/PMID above): case/control ratio 44:16, making predictive accuracy prevalence-sensitive. Group age 3–12 and sex/GI characteristics previously quoted from Table 1 are **UNVERIFIED in this round's directly opened abstract**.
- **Dataset-level VERIFIED (repository, not literature citation):** Ding/David V4 and Zou V3–V4, different sites, age/sex only in David, provisional labels Ding/Zou. In Round 2, Zou's raw genus-count depth separates groups at direct AUC around 0.93 when *lower* depth predicts ASD, while a trained source-cohort depth model assigns reversed direction (AUROC ~0.07). This is a sample/batch confounding warning, not causal proof. See `method.md` and `diagnostics.csv`.
