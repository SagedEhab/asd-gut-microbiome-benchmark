# Can a child's gut-microbial profile be used to predict ASD?

**Round-01 answer (research use only):** A gut-microbial profile can contain information that discriminates sampled ASD and control children in some datasets, but **the evidence in this round is insufficient to claim robust out-of-cohort prediction**. Nothing here is a diagnosis or clinically validated.

We fitted three methods to 331 samples from three public 16S rRNA cohorts, always holding out one entire cohort. On **Ding (122 children)**, abundance/network/combined AUROCs were **0.477/0.506/0.483**; on **Zou (92)**, **0.523/0.774/0.730**; and on **David (117 siblings)**, **0.579/0.591/0.605**. These contrasting results are more informative than the best single score. Fixed training-derived network projections improved transfer to Zou, but not consistently to Ding or David. A constant baseline yields 0.5 AUROC. Label-permutation controls were close to 0.5 on average and variable. Confidence intervals, detailed methods and code are provided in the accompanying files. A metadata-only comparison was not estimable because age and sex are missing in both Ding and Zou.

Published studies also support caution. A study selecting features in one cohort reported cross-validated AUROCs of approximately 0.748 and 0.740 in two additional cohorts, which should not be conflated with testing a fully frozen fitted model (DOI:10.1038/s41598-023-50601-7). An independent shotgun-metagenomics validation reported AUROC 0.734 (DOI:10.1080/19490976.2024.2418984). A careful analysis of 247 children found that autism-associated dietary selectivity could account for many microbiome differences (DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2021.10.015). Thus transfer performance is mixed and potential confounding substantial.

**What would change my mind:** several geographically and technically independent, prospectively specified cohorts showing consistently above-chance AUROC with narrow intervals, pre-frozen pipelines, no family or site leakage, and improvement over age, sex, diet, gastrointestinal symptom and batch baselines.

**Next round:** harmonize sample-level age/sex/diet/GI metadata and verify labels; add additional public 16S cohorts; quantify sequencing-depth and assay sensitivity; assess family-aware label permutation, confidence/calibration, and study-to-study heterogeneity. Pre-register model-selection criteria and reserve all final holdout scoring until the end.
