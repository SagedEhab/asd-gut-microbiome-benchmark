## 3. Eight Xu-2019 genera: direction and numerical evidence

**References:** **Xu et al. 2019**, DOI **10.3389/fpsyt.2019.00473**, PMID **31404299**, **VERIFIED**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6673757/ (nine included studies, 254 ASD). **Iglesias-Vázquez et al. 2020**, DOI **10.3390/nu12030792**, PMID **32192218**, **VERIFIED**, https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/12/3/792 (18 studies, 493 ASD/404 controls). **The numerical percentages and separate 95% CIs below are pooled *group abundances*, NOT confidence intervals of ASD-control differences or SMDs; authors' ratio is ASD/control point-estimate ratio.** Xu's genus taxonomy sometimes uses the species *E. coli*, not the modern composite 16S genus `Escherichia-Shigella`.

| Taxon | Xu 2019 direction (**VERIFIED** where stated) | Iglesias-Vázquez 2020 and verified numerical intervals | Consistent direction? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Akkermansia** | Lower; Xu reports ASD **0.1%** vs control **0.2%**, ratio **0.50**, **P=0.658**; Xu's quoted CI formatting is not interpretable on the % scale, hence numeric CI **UNVERIFIED**. | Descriptively lower but **not significant**; exact group intervals **UNVERIFIED** in retrieved text. | **Directional tendency**, weak evidence; no reliable significant effect. |
| **Bacteroides** | **Lower** (Xu Discussion/Abstract); Xu difference SMD/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | **Higher** ASD **9.04% [7.62,10.47]** vs controls **4.69% [3.67,5.71]**, ratio **1.93**, between-subgroup I² **95.8%** (Table 2, section 3.4.1). **VERIFIED**. | **No — reversal across meta-analyses.** |
| **Bifidobacterium** | Lower; SMD/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | **Lower** ASD **0.46% [0.33,0.59]** vs controls **0.89% [0.72,1.05]**, ratio **0.52**, subgroup I² **93.9%** (section 3.5.2). **VERIFIED**. | Broadly same sign, **extremely heterogeneous**. |
| **Parabacteroides** | Lower (Xu Abstract), SMD/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | **Higher** ASD **0.32% [0.21,0.43]** vs controls **0.04% [approximately 0.00,0.08]** (Table 2); ratio **8.0**, I² **95.3%** (section 3.4.2). **VERIFIED** for Table 2; note article prose displays an **inconsistent control interval (0.08–0.15)**, so do not resolve it without original data. | **No — reversal**, plus publication-internal interval discrepancy. |
| **Enterococcus** | Lower (Xu Discussion/Abstract); specific pooled difference/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | Not established as a robust direction for this genus in retrieved 2020 sections; **UNVERIFIED**. | **Insufficient**. |
| **Escherichia-Shigella** | Xu reports *Escherichia coli* lower; this is **not equivalent** to SILVA `Escherichia-Shigella`; genus-composite effect/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | Genus-composite direction and CI **UNVERIFIED**. | **Not established for the requested taxonomic feature.** |
| **Faecalibacterium** | Higher; Xu ASD **10.2% [3.9,16.6]** vs controls **7.7% [2.1,13.3]**, ratio **1.32** (Xu section Faecalibacterium). **VERIFIED** after converting fractional CIs ×100. | **Higher** ASD **6.84% [5.34,8.35]** vs control **5.00% [4.15,5.85]**, ratio **1.37**, between-group I² **77%** (section 3.4.3). **VERIFIED**. | **Broadly same sign**, still variable. |
| **Lactobacillus** | Higher (Xu Discussion/Abstract), SMD/CI **UNVERIFIED**. | No confident pooled effect or 95% interval available from the accessed 2020 table; **UNVERIFIED**. | **Not confirmed across meta-analyses.** |

**Critical interpretation:** There is **no consistent eight-genus directional signature**. Bacteroides and Parabacteroides reverse between these two reviews, and relative abundances are not exchangeable with standardized mean differences. No fabricated SMDs, effect-difference CIs or absent taxon-specific statistics are supplied; recovering forest-plot inputs is a next-round task.
